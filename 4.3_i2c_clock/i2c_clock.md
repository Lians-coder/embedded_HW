# 4.3 I²C Clock

## Features

- Initialization and correct operation of the SSD1306 display (128×64).
- Reading the current time from DS1307 (I²C).
- Displaying the date and time in the EEE DD.MM.YYYY HH:MM:SS format.
- Displaying the temperature under the time from the DS18B20 (OneWire).
- Animation of seconds (dots).
